drunk poetry

You should never write poems when you are drunk After all, you're not really at your most pithy But when sobriety's gone and your heart's been sunk Well these are the moments you feel all word-smithy. Too much bourbon has provided too much fuel For your stories of romance and treason But it's also drowned out any semblance of cool And though you've got rhyme, there's no reason. I get it, you want to express all your woe And document drama occurring Your verse rivals Byron! Tennyson! Poe! Except that it's clumsy and slurring.
